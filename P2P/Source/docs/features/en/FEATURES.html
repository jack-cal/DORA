SUMUWW P2P Full / Light 0.3.6

P2P supports fees based on the current queue, manual rates and reviewed fee increases. Unsent canceled previews release reserved funds. Refunds require only the chain holding your funds. Agreed amounts and fee reserves stay fixed.

1 yugeon = 0.00000001 SMW

Deposit SMW to sell or BTC to buy, including network fees, and wait for confirmations. Order funding is allocated locally and checked again before trading; allocation is not on-chain escrow. Titles and descriptions are removed. The Trade button beside an order opens the exchange. Automatic discovery, reconnection and bidirectional TLS 1.3 replace manual counterparty IP entry. Internet discovery needs reachable updated peers; it does not bypass every router or firewall. Both trading programs must be updated.
