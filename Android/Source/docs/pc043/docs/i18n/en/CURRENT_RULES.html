Whitepaper summary and current rules

SUMUWW · 2026-10-26 · 2.3

SUMUWW is an independent SHA-256d chain using UTXOs. One SMW equals 100,000,000 yugeon. A miner may claim new issuance plus the fees of included transactions. Fees transfer existing coins; they do not increase maximum issuance. Mining history separates these block totals from your received amount. Automatic fees use the current local queue; manual rates use yugeon/vB. The target block interval is 600 seconds, not a confirmation promise.

From source block 9,600, activity responses and approval by more than two thirds of the anchored generation certify the candidate list. The existing beacon selects three witnesses; the event probability remains 20%. A pending job can renew its witness round after activation while preserving its A/B pair and event beacon. Prior replies remain archived and cannot be mixed across rounds. The certificate does not prove independent people or global reachability. Setup, selected-job and completed-proof counts are different; 11 setup records minus 9 selected jobs does not mean 2 completed proofs.

Certified active-witness selection is required from source block 9,600. Earlier records remain intact. Update nodes and wallets before that height: older versions can choose a different chain when work is equal. Pending jobs keep their history.
